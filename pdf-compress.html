How it works

For every page we compare two candidates and keep the smaller one: (a) a lossless structural copy of the original page (keeps text sharp and selectable — ideal for reports and contracts), and (b) a re-encoded image version (only wins for scanned pages with heavy photos). If the original page is already optimal, it is kept as-is — your PDF will never get bigger.

Typical results: scanned documents shrink 50–90%; text-only reports may shrink little (they are already efficient) but never grow.

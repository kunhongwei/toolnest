What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin from a work by Cicero (c. 45 BC), used as placeholder text in publishing and design since the 1500s. It's used because it looks like natural language — reasonable word lengths and punctuation — without distracting readers with real meaning.

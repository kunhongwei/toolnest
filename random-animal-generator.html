About this tool

This random animal picker draws from a curated list of 100+ animals across mammals, birds, reptiles, fish and insects. Every result comes with a short fun fact, so it works great as an icebreaker, a drawing prompt, a "guess the animal" game, or a creative writing spark.

Frequently asked: How random is it? Each animal in the selected category has an equal chance. Can I use it offline? Yes — everything runs in your browser, no data is sent anywhere.
